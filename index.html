About

Technology built for the outdoor living industry.

Immagine Studios is a software company focused on transforming how outdoor environments are designed, visualized, and sold. We develop tools that combine spatial technology, artificial intelligence, and real-time visualization to help professionals work more effectively.

Our platform is designed to compress the time between a site visit and a signed proposal — giving sales and design teams a more powerful, professional experience from the first conversation with a homeowner.

We are currently in active development, working closely with industry partners to bring our first product to market.

What We Build

Three capabilities, one seamless workflow.

Spatial Measurement

Our software uses mobile AR to capture accurate measurements of an outdoor space during a site visit — no manual takeoffs or return trips required.

AI-Assisted Design

Artificial intelligence helps generate and refine outdoor living designs in real time, giving professionals a head start and homeowners an immediate visual to react to.

Proposals & Pricing

The platform generates professional proposals with real-time pricing, so a sales team can leave a homeowner's property with a complete, signed agreement in hand.

Company

Building with purpose from day one.

Immagine Studios was founded in 2025 with a clear thesis: the outdoor living industry deserves purpose-built software that reflects how professionals actually work. Most design and sales tools used today were built for other industries and adapted imperfectly.

We are a small, focused team combining deep experience in software, sales technology, and spatial computing. We work with a select group of early industry partners to make sure what we build solves real problems in the real world.

Our approach is deliberate. Rather than building broadly and quickly, we are developing a complete, production-grade platform that is reliable, easy to use in the field, and designed to scale across an organization of any size.

Immagine Studios, LLC is a registered limited liability company operating in the United States.
